How is cooling need assessed?

The four inputs are population density, socioeconomic disadvantage (SEIFA IRSD), vegetation need (NDVI), and land surface temperature. Individual indicator maps show each input separately.

Why a 250 m grid?

Each 250 m × 250 m cell summarises neighbourhood-scale conditions consistently. Conditions may vary within a cell, so rooftop polygons are assessed separately.

These are relative priority scores, not direct temperature predictions.

METHOD AND LIMITATIONS

How the green-roof map works

This planning prototype links neighbourhood cooling need, screened rooftop opportunities, and illustrative implementation scenarios for Inner Melbourne.

1. Identify cooling need

Population density, SEIFA socioeconomic vulnerability, NDVI vegetation need, and land surface temperature are assessed on a nominal 250 m grid. Balanced uses the original combined score. Alternative planning focuses assign 40% weight to a selected indicator and 20% to each other indicator.

2. Prioritise candidate roofs

Screened candidate rooftops combine a neighbourhood need score with an area score. Scenario 1 selects scores ≥4.5 (Very High); Scenario 2 selects ≥4.0 (High and Very High); Scenario 3 selects ≥3.0 (Moderate and above). Different planning focuses result in different selected rooftops.

3. Estimate potential cooling

Selected rooftop geometry is dissolved to avoid double-counting, intersected with grid cells, and summed by cell. The model uses selected rooftop area divided by a nominal 62,500 m² cell area. Below 30% coverage, the model provides no cooling estimate. Between 30% and 90%, estimated cooling rises linearly from 1.0 °C to 3.8 °C; above 90% it is capped at 3.8 °C.

4. Interpret comparisons

The scenario chart counts 250 m grid cells with a positive modelled reduction. Average temperature drop is calculated only across affected cells, not over the entire study area. The outcomes describe illustrative land-surface temperature changes, not measured changes in air temperature.

Limitations

Roof screening and available elevation/roof information are incomplete. Structural capacity, drainage, waterproofing, ownership, cost and access need independent verification. Cooling coefficients, weights, thresholds and fixed cell-area assumptions introduce uncertainty. The map does not establish site feasibility or guarantee cooling.
