About the prototype

Green Roof Implementation Support Map

For planners: first identify neighbourhoods with greater cooling need, then shortlist rooftop candidates and compare progressively broader implementation scenarios.

The neighbourhood need analysis uses 250 m × 250 m grid cells. Candidate roofs are shown at building scale. Population, SEIFA IRSD, NDVI and surface temperature inform the combined need score.

The map supports prioritisation and further investigation rather than automatic implementation decisions.

Scenario outputs: cooling is illustrative and literature-derived, not a calibrated prediction of future local air temperature.
